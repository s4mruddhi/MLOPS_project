# Business Travel and Expense Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the process for requesting business travel and claiming eligible travel expenses.

## Travel Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel.

Travel requests should include the business purpose, destination, expected travel dates, and estimated expenses.

Employees should use the organization's approved travel booking process whenever available.

## Transportation

Economy-class air travel is the standard option for business trips.

Higher-class travel requires an approved business exception.

Employees should select reasonably priced transportation options that meet the business requirement.

## Accommodation

Employees may claim reasonable hotel expenses for approved business travel.

The selected accommodation should be appropriate for the business location and within the organization's applicable travel limits.

## Meals

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel may be reimbursed according to the applicable expense limits.

Employees must retain itemized receipts where required.

## Expense Claims

Expense claims should be submitted through the organization's expense management system after the trip.

The claim should include the travel dates, business purpose, expense category, amount, and required supporting receipts.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, and expenses unrelated to the approved business trip are not normally reimbursable.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require documented approval from the appropriate manager or finance authority.
