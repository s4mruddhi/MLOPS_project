# VPN Access and Troubleshooting Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how employees can connect to the organization's corporate VPN and resolve common connection problems.

## VPN Access

Employees must use their organization-issued account to authenticate to the corporate VPN.

Multi-factor authentication is required when connecting from an external network.

Employees should use the approved VPN client provided by the IT department.

## Connection Procedure

1. Open the approved VPN client.
2. Select the corporate VPN profile.
3. Enter the organization-issued username and password.
4. Complete the multi-factor authentication challenge.
5. Confirm that the VPN client reports a connected status.

## Troubleshooting

If the VPN connection fails, employees should first verify that their internet connection is working.

The employee should then restart the VPN client and attempt the connection again.

If the problem continues, the employee should restart the device and retry the connection.

Employees should not disable endpoint security software to resolve VPN problems.

## Password Problems

Employees must not share their passwords with IT personnel or other employees.

If authentication fails because of an incorrect password, the employee should use the organization's approved password-reset process.

## Support

If the problem remains unresolved after the recommended troubleshooting steps, the employee should contact the IT Service Desk and provide the error message and approximate time of the failed connection.

IT support may request additional diagnostic information when investigating the incident.
